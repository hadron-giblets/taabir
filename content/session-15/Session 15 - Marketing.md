Three ideas from this session: marketing as alignment-seeking rather than persuasion, why "everyone" isn't actually an audience, and why the specific gaps in one specific place matter more than general community needs.

## The notes

**[[Alignment-Seeking, Not Broadcasting]]** — The question shifts from "how do we make people want this" to "who already wants this, and how do we reach them."

**[[The Everyone Paradox - Personas and Specificity]]** — Personas are empathy tools, not targeting tools. The welcoming room needs to speak to someone specific first and stay open enough that everyone else recognizes themselves in it. Specificity extends to place: what's actually missing within walking distance.

**[[A Message in Their Own Words]]** — Messaging is distilled from real conversations,. A member testimonial will do more work than any tagline staff could write.
