# Session 7: Vision

Vision misalignment is likely the most common failure mode for a Next Economy organization. Getting a team aligned on impact vision matters more, and comes before, almost anything else. Goals work as compass points here, not fixed destinations. The process of getting aligned is itself part of the outcome. You need to face the friction.

There's a real scope question for Taabir: at what point are there too many functions for one building to hold? The honest answer is that this gets figured out sequentially, as a new organization in a new space finds its priorities. Start with the room.

Taabir's vision isn't "house every possible function." It's "a free, welcoming place to belong." Vocational school, cooperative incubator, café, theater: all of that serves the vision, but none of it is the vision. Those are strategies. 

Think of vision as the destination, and strategy as the vehicle that gets you there. Avoid the trap where strategy is confused with vision, which is exactly how scope creep happens. A clearly held vision lets strategies get sequenced, deferred, or dropped entirely without it feeling like an identity crisis.

The site strategy's phasing (welcoming room, then earned revenue, then the café, then the theater) functions as vision discipline as much as financial planning. Each phase is a check on whether the next function actually serves the vision or distracts from it. This discipline is a practical application of [[Bylaws#Accountable Solidarity]], ensuring the organization doesn't over-extend itself and fail the community it serves.

But if Taabir incubates cooperatives, those cooperatives have their own visions, and Taabir's vision can't absorb them into itself. Taabir's actual job is creating the conditions for other people's visions to emerge. The incubator is soil, not the plant growing in it.

For the next year, LIFT Economy recommends five goals at most. A reasonable guess at what those are for Taabir: secure a site (or position for land banking), get to know the South Irving community, open the welcoming room, hire and onboard the team, and establish the first real partnership.
