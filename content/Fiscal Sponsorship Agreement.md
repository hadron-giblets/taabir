# Fiscal Sponsorship Agreement

*TAABIR USA is not its own legal entity. It is a fiscally sponsored project of its sponsor, TAABIR (EIN 87-4127679), an organization that shares the Taabir name and lineage (linked to Taabir Pakistan). This is the relationship the IRS sees. The document below reproduces the template the agreement is in. Every drafting tag left in. The map between all three documents is [[Three Governance Documents]].*

## How to Read It

- **§3 is the legal source of staff self-governance.** Our bylaws assert worker self-direction. Contractually, that authority is *delegated,* granted to the project's worker-collective by this agreement, terminable by either party on ninety days' notice.
- **§5 is the veto.** Every grant agreement and formal contract must be executed by the sponsor, and the project must comply with written requests to cease any activity that might jeopardize the sponsor's 501(c)(3). Compare the bylaws, whose Board Decision Matrix states in writing that the Board does not hold a veto ([[Bylaws of Taabir USA#X. Board Decision Matrix]]). The board we can see is powerless by design but here they are given power by contract.
- **§7 is the exit ramp.** Ninety days' notice, remaining funds to a successor 501(c)(3), and if TAABIR USA incorporates as its own 501(c)(3), it may serve as its own successor. Then we look at [[Bylaws of Taabir USA|bylaws]] as what would come next.
- **§6 makes even this garden project property.** Intellectual property created by project workers stays with the project, subject to its own worker-governance rules. Publishing openly is contractually safe.

# FISCAL SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT

*Comprehensive Worker-Directed Project Management Agreement*

THIS FISCAL SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is entered into effective as of this ________ day of ________________, 20____, by and between:

TAABIR (EIN: 87-4127679), a [State] nonprofit corporation exempt from federal income tax under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (the "Sponsor");

AND

TAABIR USA, a fiscally sponsored project operating as a worker-directed nonprofit (the "Project").

## Recitals: The Worker-Directed Relationship

This Agreement establishes a Model A (Comprehensive) fiscal sponsorship relationship. TAABIR USA operates as a programmatic activity of the Sponsor. Unlike traditional hierarchical models, this relationship is built on Worker Self-Direction. While the Sponsor's Board of Directors retains ultimate legal, tax, and fiduciary oversight, the programmatic, strategic, and operational decisions are delegated to the Project's workers to foster workplace democracy and mission-aligned empowerment.

## 1. Mission Alignment and Purpose

The Sponsor is organized for charitable purposes including [Insert Sponsor Mission]. The Project is established to [Insert Project Mission]. The Sponsor has determined that sponsoring the Project will further its own tax-exempt purposes and mission.

## 2. Legal and Tax Status

The Project is not a separate legal entity. All Project activities shall be conducted in the name of the Sponsor. The Sponsor agrees to receive grants, contributions, and gifts for the Project's purposes and will report such funds as the Sponsor's income for tax and financial purposes [cite: 1, 4, 7, 9].

## 3. Delegated Governance and Management

Authority to manage the programmatic activities and daily operations of the Project is hereby delegated to the Project's worker-collective. Project workers shall have the autonomy to determine internal governance, personnel structures, and strategic initiatives, provided they remain consistent with the Sponsor's legal and financial policies [cite: 1, 7].

## 4. Financial Administration and Fees

The Sponsor shall maintain a separate restricted fund for the Project. To defray administrative costs (accounting, legal oversight, and insurance), the Project agrees to an administrative fee of ________% on all income received [cite: 1, 4, 7]. The Sponsor will provide monthly financial reports to the Project within [15/30] days of month-end [cite: 1, 7].

## 5. Fundraising and Compliance

The Project may solicit funds earmarked for its activities. However, all grant agreements and formal contracts must be executed by the Sponsor [cite: 1, 4, 7, 9]. The Project agrees to comply with all written requests from the Sponsor to cease any activity that might jeopardize the Sponsor's 501(c)(3) status [cite: 1, 7].

## 6. Intellectual Property

Any tangible or intangible property, including copyrights and trademarks, created by the Project workers in the course of their duties shall remain the property of the Project, subject to the Project's internal worker-governance rules [cite: 4, 5, 9].

## 7. Termination and Succession

Either party may terminate this Agreement upon 90 days' written notice [cite: 1, 7, 9]. Upon termination, remaining funds shall be transferred to a Successor 501(c)(3) organization approved by both parties. If the Project incorporates as an independent 501(c)(3), it may serve as its own Successor [cite: 1, 4, 7, 9].
