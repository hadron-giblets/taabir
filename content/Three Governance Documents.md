# The Three Documents

Taabir has three governance documents at its core that are still taking shape. The reality is that with a three-member staff, Taabir operates under startup provisions and a fairly informal structure.

We have to ask, will it work for us when we try to implement all of it? What resources will it take to uphold our values? 

Let's take stock of what we have:

## The Covenant

The [[Bylaws|Community Covenant]] is the values layer. It came out of the TAABIR community gathering on March 22, 2026 in a full day of conversations and visioning and voting. This document defines the groups of people in charge and their roles, consent-based decisions, abundance practice, time as equity, burnout named as a structural failure. 

It is the layer we operate from week to week in startup mode. It was generated from Taabir's community, and so its expressed values guide our decisions and processes.

## The Bylaws

The [[Bylaws of Taabir USA|legal bylaws]] are the constitutional layer, drafted April 2026 off the [Sustainable Economies Law Center](https://www.theselc.org/) template. 

This document defines structure in a more legally legible way: board of three to nine that cannot direct operations. A decision matrix where staff hold operational power and the Board's own document says, in writing, that it does not hold a veto. Whistleblower protections, document retention, conflict of interest stuff that a tax authority looks for.

Much of the structure here is structure we don't currently do anything with. The Board it describes has never been put together. These bylaws are not a record of how Taabir is governed but they are the shape of how Taabir promises to be governed when we might need to be presented to a legal body. 

It's an imposed structure, in one way. It's written with legal necessity, but it's a lot better than inventing a constitution from scratch.

## The Fiscal Sponsorship

The third document is the [[Fiscal Sponsorship Agreement]]. Taabir USA operates under Taabir (linked to Taabir Pakistan) as a fiscal sponsor, which means the IRS does not see Taabir USA's board at all. It sees the sponsor's board. The sponsor files the returns, holds the compliance obligations, and, crucially! *carries the liability*. Every dollar of fiduciary exposure that a board normally absorbs, the sponsor's directors absorb now. 

At Taabir's community gathering, there was a lot of negative feelings about what a board implies. Big power structure. No one in attendance wanted to be a Board Member in such terms. So most powers reside with Staff.

But the board is also the people financially on the hook for the organization. There's risk involved, and insurance, and liability. The concept of a Board sounds powerful but it is also a vulnerable place to be.

## The Gaps (a field guide)

Reading the three documents against each other, you'll find questions:

- **The blank certificate.** The bylaws are drafted, not adopted, never sent to the IRS. Do we aspire to move out of the fiscal sponsorship and incorporate using our own documents some day?
- **Duties without shields.** The bylaws give directors duty of care and grounds for removal but offer no indemnification article, no D&O insurance provision, nothing that protects the humans holding the liability.
- **The ghost Executive Director.** The whistleblower policy routes retaliation complaints to "the Executive Director," an office that appears nowhere else in the document. In a worker self-directed nonprofit, who is that, really? What is mediation within the organization?
- **The matrix vs. the sponsor.** The decision matrix assigns the Board exclusive authority over budgets, bylaws amendments, and dissolution. Under fiscal sponsorship, some of that authority may actually sit with the sponsor today.

## Why this is the good kind of mess

The covenant tells you who we are. The bylaws tell you what we've envisioned we may become. The sponsorship tells you who signs, today. They all hold principles of the org and are an evolving shape decided heavily by the resources we have to be faithful to these rules and the South Irving community.
