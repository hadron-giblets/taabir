# De-Risking Through Relationship: Fiscal Sponsorship and the Capital Stack

Most funders want de-risking before they'll commit, and a capital stack is how an organization gets there without any single funder or instrument carrying the whole burden: city grants, subordinated debt, community investment, and a philanthropic loan loss reserve can all layer together, each one making the others more feasible. Before pursuing any of it, the discipline is to qualify the funder as rigorously as the funder qualifies the applicant: what have they funded before, at what scale, what do they expect in return, what does their own disclosure reveal about how they actually operate.

Taabir USA operating under parent Taabir's existing 501(c)(3) status is a structural asset in exactly this sense. An established parent organization provides credibility, track record, and existing governance infrastructure, and for funders who require de-risking, which is most of them, the relationship with that entity is itself a form of risk reduction before a single grant is written.

The Irving Redevelopment Land Bank represents a potential layer in the same stack: city-conveyed property functions effectively as a grant, and it is the layer that reduces acquisition cost, making every subsequent instrument, a CDFI mortgage, philanthropic down payment support, community pre-sales, more feasible in turn. Understanding how these layers interact with each other, not just with Taabir individually, is the fundraising work ahead.
