Four ideas from this session: why the order you pursue funding in matters as much as how much you raise, how an existing relationship can de-risk an entire capital stack, why the same organization has to speak two financial languages to two different audiences, and why some funders structurally cannot fund the thing that actually matters.

## The notes

**[[The Order of Operations - Growing From Operations First]]** — Start with lighter obligation funding sources before heavier ones: operations first, then partner resources, non-dilutive funding, community investment, and investors last.

**[[De-Risking Through Relationship - Fiscal Sponsorship and the Capital Stack]]** — What are the layers of a capital stack, from the parent 501(c)(3) to land-bank property reducing acquisition cost.

**[[Two Languages, One Community - The Framing Challenge and the Obligation Question]]** — Every dollar carries obligations beyond repayment and framing it for your funder plays a big role.

**[[Funders Who Can't See It - Compensation as Mission Drift]]** — Some funders don't see paychecks as impact. Those are not funders suited to work with Taabir.
