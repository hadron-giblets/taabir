Three ideas from this session: designing roles around tasks instead of titles, protecting the important-but-not-urgent work that is an organization's future, and treating personal sustainability as an organizational requirement rather than an individual problem.

## The notes

**[[Tasks, Not Titles - Task-Based Design and Trust]]** — Roles as sets of tasks across design, revenue, and admin areas, with accountability running to the task rather than a manager.. Trust and structure have to be built together.

**[[Protecting the Future - Org Rhythms and the Design Layer]]** — Scheduled rhythms exist to protect the important design layer — vision, culture, strategy — from being consumed by the urgent work.

**[[The Redundancy of Care - Lead-Support and Personal Sustainability]]** — Lead-support pairs and documentation prevent single points of failure, and the same logic applied to people makes rest a scheduled thing rather than a leftover. Burnout kills too many organizations.
