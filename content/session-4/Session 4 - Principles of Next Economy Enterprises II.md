The second half of LIFT Economy's ten principles (6–10), picking up where [[Session 3 - Principles of Next Economy Enterprises I|part one]] left off. Part one was mostly about ownership and decision-making. This half is about knowledge, ecology, power, and people: how an enterprise teaches, how it operates ecologically, how it relates to power outside itself, and how it treats the humans who keep it alive.

## The notes

**[[Principle 6 - Open Source and Regionally Replicable|Principle 6: Open Source and Regionally Replicable]]** — Innovation as a contribution to the commons; growth means the model replicating in new places, not one organization expanding. This digital garden is itself an instance of the principle.

**[[Principle 7 - Transparency]]** — Information as a tool of distributed power rather than control. Redundancy audits and participatory budgeting keep knowledge from pooling at the top.

**[[Principle 8 - Regenerative Operations]]** — Beyond doing less harm: leaving the land and community healthier. The building as textbook — rainwater, healthy materials, the vocational school practicing on its own walls — and the renting-versus-owning tension underneath.

**[[Principle 9 - Supporting Movement Building for Broader Systems Change|Principle 9: Supporting Movement Building]]** — Playing a bigger game than the service: making the solidarity model effective enough that other institutions adopt it. The Community Connector role is the engine.

**[[Principle 10 - Supporting Personal Growth and Development]]** — The workplace as a site of human development, with burnout defined as structural failure — and the honest note that wanting this has to be balanced as the org builds.
