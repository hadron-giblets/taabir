Three ideas from this session: the developmental stages a team moves through as it learns shared-power decisionmaking, why consent-based governance is both simpler and culturally harder than hierarchy, and how a decision zone chart protects people from having to be in every decision.

## The notes

**[[The Seven Stages of Cooperativism]]** — Shared power participation is a developmental arc, not a switch: silence, cautious input, weighing in on everything including pizza toppings, overcorrection, discouragement, discernment, and finally facilitation.

**[[Simple Mechanism, Hard Culture - Consent vs. Hierarchy]]** — Consent governance is simpler than any approval ladder and culturally harder.

**[[The Decision Zone as Protection - Board Decision Matrix]]** — The Board Decision Matrix maps authority in advance so nobody has to be in every decision and decisions don't default to whoever happens to be in the room.
