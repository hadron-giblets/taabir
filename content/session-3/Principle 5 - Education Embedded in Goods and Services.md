# Principle 5: Education Embedded in Goods and Services

**The Thesis:** Teach people how to do what you do. Turn consumers into producer-consumers. The ultimate goal is to put yourself out of business by making the community self-sufficient enough that they no longer need you.

**The Bylaw:** Stated purpose as "charitable, religious, educational, and scientific" ([[Bylaws#IX. Amending This Covenant]]). The vocational training function, the cooperative business incubator, and the open-sourcing of knowledge: "knowledge produced by TAABIR shall be shared openly" ([[Bylaws#Abundance Practice]]).

**The Application:** Taabir is built on this principle rather than adding it on top. It is not a community center that happens to teach; it is a teaching institution that happens to gather people. The bylaws are public, the models are meant to be copied, and the goal is to spawn other nodes. Success is not Taabir closing, but Taabir becoming one node among many in a city-wide network of cooperative spaces and vocational hubs.
