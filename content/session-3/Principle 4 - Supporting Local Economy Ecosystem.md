# Principle 4: Supporting Local Economy Ecosystem

**The Thesis:** Money that stays local keeps circulating instead of leaving the neighborhood. Local ownership alone is not enough, as a locally-owned business can still extract value. The real decision happens in procurement and banking choices.

**The Bylaw:** "TAABIR shall not compete against peer organizations for grants when collaboration is possible" ([[Bylaws#Abundance Practice]]). The Ecological Commitment prefers vendors and materials aligned with ecological sustainability, "even when such preference requires additional effort or nominal additional cost" ([[Bylaws#Ecological Commitment]]).

**The Application:** Taabir does not just buy local; it refuses to compete against its own ecosystem for funding. This is a structural commitment to the health of peer organizations, not just a sentiment. The Ecological Commitment is intentionally vague right now (see [[The Density Problem - Scale and Connection|the seed this could grow into]]), but a future, sharper version would create real demand for the cooperatives Taabir itself helps start. Procurement functions as ecosystem design.
