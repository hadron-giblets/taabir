# Principle 1: Need-Oriented Basic Goods & Services

**The Thesis:** Communities define their own needs. The role of an economy is to meet those needs, not to maximize profit for whoever is selling. A [[Solidarity Economy|next economy enterprise]] exists to serve a need, rather than using "meeting needs" as a mere mechanism for growth.

**The Bylaw:** "TAABIR is an ecosystem for the people of Dallas, functioning as a resource center for vocational training, cooperative business, and the solidarity economy, a co-working space for member organizations and businesses, and a community space for underserved groups." ([[Bylaws#I. Mission Statement]])

**The Application:** The community did not ask for a vocational school. They asked for a place. Vocational training, the co-working space, and the café all sit downstream of that first, smaller ask. Taabir's first function, before training, before revenue, and before anything else, is unconditional belonging. That is a need-oriented good in its purest form. The first thing Taabir offers is free.
