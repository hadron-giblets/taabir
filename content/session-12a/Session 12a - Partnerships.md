Three ideas from this session: partnerships as a form of capital, the different ecologies of partnership Taabir sits inside at once, and why Taabir may need to be deliberately more than one thing depending on who it's partnering with.

## The notes

**[[Partnership as Substitution - Capital Without Money]]** — Partnerships are capital when the value exchanged is understood broadly: capability, reach, credibility, practices that interlock. A credit union means ethical banking without building a bank. A housing co-op means stable housing without becoming a landlord.

**[[The Partnership Ecologies - Four Tiers of Trust and Friction]]** — Trust and friction apply in different ratios to different partnerships. Watch the power structures they produce.

**[[Deliberately Multiple - Different Partnerships, Different Organizations]]** — Different partnerships create different versions of the same organization. Audience adjustment, not dishonesty.
