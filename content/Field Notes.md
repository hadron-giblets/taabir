These are my field notes. One geologist's read of LIFT Economy's NextEconomy MBA, cross-pollinated with homework, lesson plans, and the later thoughts that grew out of them — collected here because Taabir is being built out of them. A living document: the dates are real, the notes get tended, and the mapping is still in progress.

## Three ways in

**The path.** Eighteen sessions in two arcs: fall (1–9) is foundations, spring (10–18) is operations. Start at [[Session 1 - Economy as Need Meeting]] or wherever your syllabus happens to be; the ← → at the foot of every session page walks the course in order. If a week compresses, the session's intro plus its one embedded note — "if you read one" — is the whole week in ten minutes.

**The threads.** Every note carries its threads at the top of the page. Click one and the garden reorganizes itself by problem instead of by week — governance, money, positioning, conflict, research. When you're stuck on a specific thing, the thread is faster than the path.

**The web.** Hover any underlined link to preview where it leads without leaving your page. The right edge of every note lists its backlinks — everything that connects to the one you're reading. Search is in the top left for when you know the word; the graph is for when you only know the territory.

## The load-bearing walls

The Start Here list on the front page is the organizational layer — the bylaws, the site strategy, the fiscal sponsorship, the fellows. The garden is my read; those documents are the thing itself.

Field notes, not findings — read them the way you'd read an outcrop: any layer you start from connects to the rest.
