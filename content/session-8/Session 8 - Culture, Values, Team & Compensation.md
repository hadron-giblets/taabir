# Session 8: Culture - Values, Team & Compensation

Culture breaks an organization before finances do. This session's central tension, named by facilitator Phoenix, is a cooperative school where everyone agrees on the value "for the children." One parent buys plastic toys. Another brings children to abolition rallies. Both are living the same stated value, and the behaviors are incompatible. Some values feel too sacred to interrogate, and interrogating them anyway turns out to be exactly the necessary work.
