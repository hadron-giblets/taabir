# Interdependence, Not a Pole: The Overcorrection Trap

Culture tends to fail in one of two directions. Corporate culture fails through competition, territorialism, and individualism. Its mirror image, a cooperative culture paralyzed by consensus, fails just as completely: one person's concern can dominate indefinitely, and no tradeoff can ever get made. Neither pole is the goal. The goal is interdependence, the capacity to act autonomously inside a shared alignment, rather than either isolated self-interest or permanent deadlock.

The risk for Taabir specifically is the second failure mode: a General Circle unable to act because every voice must be fully satisfied before anything moves. The bylaws' Startup Provision addresses this directly, allowing streamlined decision-making while staff stays under six people. Taabir is still inside that window, and it needs to be, since early growth depends on the organization actually being able to move, not just agree in principle that moving would be good.
