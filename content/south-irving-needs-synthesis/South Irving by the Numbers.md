title: "South Irving by the Numbers"
---

75060, 75061, and 75062 are the three zip codes that make up South Irving, a division the city and its own residents already draw. In the 2024 Resident Survey's open comments, a cluster of write-ins asked the city to focus on this area by name, and nearly all of them came from these three zips.

The gap between North and South Irving shows up cleanly in the data. North Irving (75038, 75039, 75063) has higher incomes, newer housing stock, and its households sit only 8 to 15 percent below the ALICE survival threshold. South Irving is older, its housing stock has seen no new multifamily construction since 1990, and roughly half of its households, 48 to 54 percent, are either in poverty or working without enough income to cover a bare-bones budget in Dallas County.

75061 carries the deepest outright poverty, at 14.6 percent, with another 39.6 percent in the ALICE range: working, but below a survival budget. 75060 runs close behind at 13.8 percent poverty and 34.8 percent ALICE. 75062 has a similar ALICE share (39.9 percent) but far less poverty (7.6 percent), reading more like a transition zone, working but squeezed, rather than a zone in crisis.

Residents feel this difference too. Asked to rate quality of living relative to affordability, 75060 and 75061 come in low (43 and 38 percent positive), 75062 sits closer to the middle (60 percent), and the North Irving zips all rate significantly higher (58 to 73 percent).
