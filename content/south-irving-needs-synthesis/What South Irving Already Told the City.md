# What South Irving Already Told the City

Two surveys by the city of Irving land on much the same handful of concerns. The city's 2026 Resident Survey, aimed at the general population, put homelessness (39 percent) and economic development (28 percent) at the top of citywide concerns. The Consolidated Plan's own needs survey, aimed specifically at lower-income residents and asking about community services, found mental health services (43 percent), senior services (40 percent), and homeless services (39 percent) at the top, with childcare, services for new Americans, and a domestic violence shelter volunteered in the write-ins.

Retail and restaurant variety show up as a write-in concern in both North and South Irving at similar rates. South residents cite retail and grocery gaps at 20.8 percent and restaurants at 14.8 percent, only a few points above North Irving's numbers on the same questions. The gap that holds up, though, is social services: South Irving residents are about 1.6 times as likely as North Irving residents to name increasing social services and community resources as their top fix for the city. That is the category Taabir is best positioned to answer directly.

Two comments from the survey's open-ended section put a finer point on it.

> "There are literally NO good shopping places in the Irving area near the 183 and MacArthur area... all the restaurants and places to shop seem to be in the Las Colinas area. I think there needs to be more to do in THIS area. Area code 75062."
> — 2024 Resident Survey, open comment

> "The city needs a more diverse commercial mix by incentivizing premium dining, retail, and entertainment developments over the current saturation of service-oriented businesses like insurance agencies, tire shops, and banks."
> — 2024 Resident Survey, open comment
