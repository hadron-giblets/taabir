## Where conversations should go next

Site priority: 75060 and 75061 show the deepest strain on affordability, street condition, and retail variety. 75062 tracks closer to the citywide norm on some measures but stands out on the social-services ask. Site selection means thinking carefully about where Taabir can actually have use and impact, not just where the need is statistically deepest.

The third place pattern: Taabir cannot just be a service list. It needs something people want to move toward, a familiar, trusted place, not only a rundown of the factors squeezing them for resources.

Co-op appetite: the open question left is whether small business owners have real interest in shared purchasing, shared space, or cooperative structures, given they've already named the wage problem themselves.

*Sources: City of Irving 2024 Resident Survey (Elite Research / ETC Institute); City of Irving Consolidated Plan (2023-2028) needs assessment and stakeholder consultations, including HOME-ARP public service needs; United For ALICE Dallas County household data; informal community conversations conducted by Taabir, July 2026.*
