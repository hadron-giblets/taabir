title: "Sales as Alignment-Seeking"
---

Sales, reframed, isn't manipulation. It's alignment-seeking, asking whether real value is being created, whether the arrangement is consensual, and whether the benefit runs both ways. Education may be needed when a real need exists but the person does not yet recognize it. Disqualifying honestly, telling someone directly that the fit isn't there, is part of the same practice, not a failure of it. The relationship is expected to outlast any single transaction either way.

Part of "selling" Taabir is opportunity made visible. Someone rents a co-working desk because they see other people already building things there and want to be part of that energy, because they can envision alignment with their own goals. Someone starts a cooperative with Taabir's help because they've become familiar and want it for themselves. Taabir must be a recognizable shape for other people, something they can understand and use. The sale is in the demonstration of both the theory and the outcomes.
