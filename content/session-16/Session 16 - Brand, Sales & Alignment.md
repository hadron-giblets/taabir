Four ideas from this session: brand as something a community becomes rather than something marketed to it, sales reframed as alignment-seeking rather than persuasion, the alignment matrix as a tool for deciding where relationship energy belongs, and how that same cycle applies to funders as much as to members or tenants.

## The notes

**[[Becoming a Community, Not Marketing to One]]** — Brand community dissolves the wall between institution and the people it serves. Be part of the place you're in.

**[[Sales as Alignment-Seeking]]** — Real value, consensual arrangement, benefit that runs both ways, and honest rejection of certain types of funding.

**[[Taabir's Alignment Matrix]]** — Mission alignment crossed with current interest: prioritize, nurture, disqualify with discernment, deprioritize without guilt. The same matrix applies to a customer, a member, a tenant, or a funder.

**[[The Funder Alignment Cycle]]** — Invitation, rapport, a starting point, feedback that deepens the relationship once we're through with qualifying questions first.
