# Taabir's Alignment Matrix

The alignment matrix crosses two questions, how aligned is this person or organization with the mission, and how interested are they right now, to decide where relationship energy actually belongs. High alignment and high interest means prioritize. High alignment and low interest means nurture, through education and patience rather than pressure. Low alignment and high interest means disqualify with discernment, honestly, rather than taking the engagement just because it is there. Low alignment and low interest means deprioritize without needing to justify it further. The same matrix applies whether the person in question is a customer, a member, a tenant, or a funder.

For Taabir, high alignment and high interest describes the person who needs what Taabir offers, a free welcoming place to belong, or affordable space to build a cooperative, and who walks in and says yes almost immediately. Prioritize them, welcome them, onboard them. High alignment and low interest describes someone who would genuinely benefit from Taabir but does not yet see themselves as a "community center person." That's where the word-of-mouth campaign does its slow work: nurture, educate gently, invite without pressure. Low alignment and high interest describes someone who wants to use Taabir but whose actual needs, a traditional office lease, a one-time event rental, do not match what Taabir is built to offer. Disqualify them honestly and refer them to something conventional, preserving the relationship rather than forcing a bad fit. Low alignment and low interest simply isn't the audience, and can be released without guilt.
